English

No feed. No account. No ads. Just a camera that takes photos that look like film.

Nineteen film stocks, each with a code and a character of its own — colour negative, slide, black and white, motion picture, instant. Grain, halation and light leaks are computed per pixel, not pasted on as a texture. Free at launch: every film stock and every frame is included.

简体中文

没有信息流、没有账号、没有广告。就是一台能拍出胶片味的相机。

19 款胶卷，每一款都有代号、有性格 —— 彩色负片、反转片、黑白、电影卷、即时成像。 颗粒、漏光与光晕是逐像素算出来的，不是贴一张纹理上去。上架首发免费， 19 款胶卷与全部功能都已包含。
